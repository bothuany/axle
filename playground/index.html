What is real here, and what is not

Axle’s own

The flow canvas, the Agents dock and the overlay are the pages the app draws, driven by the app’s own ranking code. The flows, the tables and the change come from Axle’s code index run on the demo project, and the map above is the harness scan of it.

Scripted

Claude. Its steps and timing are a replay, so nothing here runs a model and nothing leaves your browser. The agent in Axle is the real Claude Code, or Codex, in a terminal.

Left out

The editor, the terminal, editing code on a card (the demo is read-only), other languages and agents. Download Axle to use them on your own code.
