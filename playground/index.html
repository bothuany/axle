What is real here, and what is not

Axle’s own

The flow canvas, the Agents dock page and the status overlay are the pages the app draws, driven by the app’s own ranking and mapping code. The flows come from Axle’s code index, run on the demo project: the HTTP? and topic? links, the tables and the change set are what it finds.

Scripted

The agent. Its words, its tool calls and its timing are a replay, so the terminal takes only the keys the replay expects, and says so for anything else. Nothing here runs a model, and nothing leaves your browser.

Left out

Editing code on a card (the demo is read-only), other agents and languages, native menus, and the Codex and custom agents. Download Axle to use them on your own code.

Axle is open source under the MIT license. It builds on VS Code.
