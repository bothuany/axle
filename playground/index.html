01 agents

Know who is working, and who is waiting for you.

Claude Code, Codex and any CLI run as the real tools, in terminals. Axle ranks them in the Agents dock and in an overlay at the top of your screen, so an agent that waits for a yes does not hide in a tab.

02 changes

See what an agent touched, not just what it typed.

When a turn ends, the dock names the flows and the table it reached. Open one and the canvas draws the change on the cards of the flow, cut down to the path that reaches it. This is that change, ready to review.

03 harness

Know what shapes your agent before it starts.

Axle scans the project for everything an agent reads or runs: instructions, rules, skills, subagents, hooks and MCP servers. This is the scan of the demo project, and it found one rule that never loads.

04 under the hood

What is real here, and what is not.

Axle’s own

The flow canvas, the Agents dock and the overlay are the pages the app draws, driven by the app’s own ranking code. The flows, the tables and the change come from Axle’s code index run on the demo project, and the map above is the harness scan of it.

Scripted

Claude. Its steps and timing are a replay, so nothing here runs a model and nothing leaves your browser. In Axle the agent is the real Claude Code, or Codex, in a terminal.

Left out

The editor, the terminal, editing code on a card (the demo is read-only), other agents and projects. Download Axle to use them on your own code.
