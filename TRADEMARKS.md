# Axle name and logo

Axle's code is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE): you may use, change and redistribute it. The licence covers the code, not the name. The name **Axle** for this editor and the Axle logo (the icons in `editor/branding/` and the marks drawn by the app) identify this project and its official releases, and stay with the project's maintainer, Recep Batuhan Dikmen.

**You may, without asking:**

- Say that your work is based on, works with or is a fork of Axle ("Built on Axle", "Fork of Axle").
- Use the name and logo to write about Axle, review it, teach it or link to it.
- Build Axle from source for yourself or inside your organisation, unchanged, and call it Axle.

**Please ask first, or use your own name and logo, if you:**

- Distribute a modified version, or a build with different defaults, to other people: give it a different name and icon, so users can tell it apart from official Axle releases, and change the identity in `product.json` (`nameShort`, `nameLong`, `applicationName`, `win32AppId`, `darwinBundleIdentifier`, the URL protocol and the data folder).
- Use the name or logo in the name of a product, service, company or domain.
- Suggest that your product or service is official, endorsed or supported by the Axle project.

If you are unsure, open an issue and ask. Claude and Codex are marks of their owners; Axle uses their names only to say which agents it works with.
