# Insilode name and logo

Insilode's code is open source under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3](LICENSE): you may use, change and redistribute it. The licence covers the code, not the name. The name **Insilode** for this editor and the Insilode logo (the icons in `editor/branding/` and the marks drawn by the app) identify this project and its official releases, and stay with the project's maintainer, Recep Batuhan Dikmen.

**You may, without asking:**

- Say that your work is based on, works with or is a fork of Insilode ("Built on Insilode", "Fork of Insilode").
- Use the name and logo to write about Insilode, review it, teach it or link to it.
- Build Insilode from source for yourself or inside your organisation, unchanged, and call it Insilode.

**Please ask first, or use your own name and logo, if you:**

- Distribute a modified version, or a build with different defaults, to other people: give it a different name and icon, so users can tell it apart from official Insilode releases, and change the identity in `product.json` (`nameShort`, `nameLong`, `applicationName`, `win32AppId`, `darwinBundleIdentifier`, the URL protocol and the data folder).
- Use the name or logo in the name of a product, service, company or domain.
- Suggest that your product or service is official, endorsed or supported by the Insilode project.

Insilode is the new name of the project that was called Axle until 0.4.x. The name has not been registered as a trademark, and the search for conflicts is not finished; this policy says how the maintainer intends to use it, not that a registration exists.

If you are unsure, open an issue and ask. Claude and Codex are marks of their owners; Insilode uses their names only to say which agents it works with.
